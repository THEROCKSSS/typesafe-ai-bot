What it does

Five jobs, every minute you're offline

Each one writes a case entry, so nothing is ever a mystery later.

🛡️automod

Chat screening

Every message is read against the server's rules in a single AI pass. Clear breaks get handled instantly; anything borderline goes straight to your DMs for a human call.

🗳️community

Reports & votes

A report needs a written reason. The AI checks the reason is real, then the community votes for 10 minutes — mute, disconnect, or no action.

⏱️spam

Spam protection

Bursts, duplicates, mention floods and link spam each get an automatic short timeout, with a channel notice so the room knows why it went quiet.

🚨nuke

Nuke defense

If an account starts mass-deleting channels, banning, or creating webhooks, their dangerous permissions are stripped immediately and every owner is alerted.

🔑permissions

Role-scoped powers

Exactly which role may warn, mute, disconnect, or configure the bot — chosen from menus, changed without a restart, owners hold everything.

📬alerts

Owner notifications

Timeouts, nuke trips, vote results, and reports needing review are DM'd to every owner — with a channel fallback if someone's DMs are closed.

How a decision is made

The AI suggests. Code decides. You can always see both.

The bot never lets a model act on its own. Every threshold, every action, and every failure mode is owned by plain code — the AI only supplies a probability.

How to try it

Four steps, no risk to anyone

Run /status

Confirms the bot is online and shows whether it's enforcing or just observing.

Run /help

You'll only see commands your role can actually use — that's the permission system working.

File a real report on a throwaway account

Watch the reason get reviewed, then let the vote run its 10 minutes. The AI reviews the outcome before anything applies.

Check /case afterwards

The whole thing — reporter, accused, reason, tally, and the AI's verdict — is sitting in the case log.
